SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This privacy policy covers the SMS notification service operated by Hillel Appel ("the service"). The service sends automated account and market alert messages to subscribers who have opted in.

Information collected

The service collects and stores only the mobile phone number provided at opt-in, used solely to deliver the requested alert messages.

No sharing of mobile information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with, sold to, rented to, or disclosed to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purpose. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Message frequency

Subscribers receive up to approximately 1–5 messages per day, depending on alert activity. Message frequency may vary.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply. Charges are billed by your mobile carrier.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to adveadbechlal@gmail.com.
