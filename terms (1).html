Program description

This SMS program, operated by Hillel Appel, sends automated account and market alert notifications (for example, price and volume alerts) to subscribers who have opted in by providing their phone number and consenting to receive text messages.

Opt-in

By providing your mobile number and agreeing to receive messages, you consent to receive recurring automated SMS alerts from this service at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies; subscribers typically receive up to 1–5 messages per day depending on alert activity.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply. Please contact your mobile carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Opt-out and help

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP, after which you will receive a final confirmation message and no further texts. Reply HELP for help, or contact adveadbechlal@gmail.com.

Supported carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Service is available on major US carriers; delivery is subject to network availability.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in data are never shared with third parties. See the Privacy Policy.
